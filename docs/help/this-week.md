# This week

**This week** is the screen Minimap opens on. It answers one question: *what needs me now?* Weeks run Monday to Sunday.

## What it shows

From the top:

- **The headline and the numbers.** Today's date and one line that says how the week stands ("3 tasks need your attention"), next to five counts: *Overdue*, *Due today*, *Blocked*, *Planned* (the rest of the week's work) and *Waiting on*. A count is coloured when there is something in it and grey at zero.
- **The seven days**, Monday to Sunday, each with the tasks due, waiting-ons expected and 1:1s that day. Today is outlined in the accent colour; a past day that still has open work due on it is **red** and says how many are late; past days are dimmed.
- **Needs attention** (red): every task that is **overdue**, **due today** or **blocked**, each once, with a pill that says why: *3 days late* (solid red), *Due today* (solid amber), *Blocked* (amber). A task can carry two (late and blocked). Late work comes first, the latest first, then today's, then blocked; a blocked task says what it is waiting for, and every row shows who it is assigned to. When there is nothing here, a green line says so.
- **Priorities this week**: the rest of the week's work, each task once: open tasks due later this week and your tasks in progress, **most important first** (priority 1 first, then the earliest due date; undated last). They are numbered, and the top three stand out, so you can see at a glance what to do next.
- **Waiting on**: open waiting-ons that are stale, overdue, or expected this week (snoozed ones stay hidden).
- **Ongoing objectives to review**: [ongoing objectives](help:objectives) whose review is overdue or falls by Sunday, with *Mark reviewed* on each.
- **1:1s this week**: 1:1 notes dated this week.

**Latest notes under each task.** Every task in *Needs attention* and in *Priorities this week* shows, under its line, the **three newest notes** that mention it (the notes you write under *Notes and findings* on the task, and any note that mentions it with `@`): the note's date and the start of its text, up to two lines, with a small *1:1* or *meeting* tag where that applies. Click a note to open it in the pane (clicking the task's own line opens the task). When a task has more than three, the last line says how many more (*+2 earlier*). Tasks with no notes show nothing extra.

"Your tasks in progress" means assigned to the person marked *you*. Late, due-today and blocked tasks are shown whoever they are assigned to.

## Doing things from here

Every row can be dealt with in place:

- The round **check** marks a task done. (If it repeats, the next one is created: see [Recurring items](help:recurring).)
- **Tomorrow** and **Next week** move the due date, and you can type any date in words (`fri`, `+3d`, `next-wed`, `2027-03-31`).
- **Resolve** closes a waiting-on, and **Snooze** hides it for three days.
- On the keyboard, `j` and `k` move between rows, `Enter` opens the item and `x` marks the highlighted task done.

## Looking at another week

The arrows next to the dates move one week back or forward, so you can look ahead at what is coming or back at what was due.

## Looking back to a past day

**Click a past day in the strip of seven days.** The screen then shows **the week as it stood on that day**: that day is "today", so what was overdue, due that day and blocked is judged against it, and the counts, the red panel and the priorities all follow. A blue bar at the top says so, and *Back to today* (or clicking today's tile, or the *This week* button) returns. The chosen day is marked *viewing* and today keeps its *today* label. The arrows then move the chosen day a week at a time, and days after today can't be clicked.

Notes are shown as they stood too: a note dated after that day is left out.

It is rebuilt from what Minimap keeps, so it is close but not a photograph: tasks you **finished since** show as open again (as *to do*, because the status they had then isn't kept), tasks you **made since** are left out, waiting-ons asked or resolved since are treated the same way, and **due dates are as they are now** (a date you moved later shows its new date). 

## If it looks empty

- Your tasks need to be **assigned to you** to appear under *My tasks*. New tasks are assigned to you by default.
- Tasks without a due date never show as due. Give them a date, or find them on the [Tasks](help:tasks) screen.
- Drive banner at the top? That is the reminder that your data is on this computer only; see [Google Drive](help:google-drive).
