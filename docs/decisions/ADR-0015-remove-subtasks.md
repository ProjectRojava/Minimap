# ADR-0015: Subtasks removed

- **Context**: subtasks (spec 29, ADR-0013) added a `subtask_of` link and, with it, groups that were scheduled from their children, a blocks-on-a-group rule, order and status hints, and frames in the dependency graph. In use they were not needed: ordinary tasks with `blocks` links already say what comes before what, and the extra concept made linking harder to explain.
- **Decision**: remove the feature end to end: the commands (`set_parent`, `create_subtask`), the panel section, the cues on cards and rows, the group rules in the schedule, impact analysis, capacity and health, the graph frames, and `core::subtasks`. Scheduling, capacity and health are back to counting every task. Spec 29 is withdrawn and ADR-0013 is superseded; their text stays as history.
- **Data compatibility**: `EdgeType::SubtaskOf` stays in the enum as a **legacy** value, allowed between no node types and ignored everywhere, so a database, backup or Drive snapshot that still holds such links keeps loading (an unknown edge type would fail to read). Migration 0012 archives the links that exist, so nothing shows or counts them; a snapshot from a device that has not upgraded may bring some back, and they stay invisible.
- **Linking instead**: linking is made easier for the tasks that exist: one **Links** section right under a task's fields (task links first, then the other links, with buttons for a new task, an existing task or something else), and a card menu with *Link a new task…* and *Link an existing task…* (spec 31).
- **Deviation**: `CLAUDE.md` §4 loses the `subtask_of` row and the matching invariant, and §5.3 the group sentence.
